v0.4.5 is mostly about environments that have no writable `/tmp`, such as Termux.

Before, anything that ran an external process failed in such an environment, and clang was reported as missing even though it was installed. The cause was that the place for temporary files was fixed at `/tmp`. The compiler now uses `$TMPDIR`, and falls back to `/tmp` when it is not set.

We also tidied the names of the files a local build produces. `make` builds `build/zevic`, `build/zevpm` and `build/zevfmt`, and the internal seed compiler moved to `build/bootstrap/zevic`.

The previous release, 0.4.4, made threads started by `Thread.Spawn` register with the GC on Windows, and made `-O0` the default. See the [releases](/en/releases/) for details.
