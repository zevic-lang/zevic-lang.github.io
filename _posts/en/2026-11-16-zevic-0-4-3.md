---
title: "Zevic 0.4.3: operators for Comparable and Equatable"
alt: /blog/2026/11/16/zevic-0-4-3/
---
In v0.4.3, comparison operators work on types you define yourself.

- On a struct or class that implements `Comparable`, `<`, `<=`, `>` and `>=` call the `Compare` method
- `==` on a struct that implements `Equatable` calls its `Equals` method. It used to compare the fields directly
- `Async.Spawn` starts its task on a detached `Thread.Thread` (`Thread` gained `Detach()`)
- Usage messages now show the `zevic` name

Version 0.4.2 had a known problem: `<` was not generated for types that implement `Comparable`. It is fixed in this version.
