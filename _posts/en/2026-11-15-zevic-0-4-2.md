v0.4.2 is the `Thread` and `BigInt` release.

- **`Zevic.Thread`**: OS threads. `Thread.Spawn(f)` starts a function on a new thread and `Join()` waits for it (calling `Join` twice is a Panic). There is also `Thread.Yield()`
- **`Zevic.BigInt`**: integers of any size, as `BigInt.BigInt`. It has `FromI64`, `Parse`, `Add`, `Sub`, `Mul`, `DivRem`, `Pow`, `ToString`, `ToI64` (an `Err` when out of range) and more, and implements `Equatable` and `Comparable`

We also brought the language specification up to date with the implementation. The main changes: concurrency uses OS threads, `Integer` was replaced by `Zevic.BigInt`, and open questions settled by the implementation were tidied up.
