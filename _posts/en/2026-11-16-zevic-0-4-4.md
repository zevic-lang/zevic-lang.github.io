---
v0.4.4 has two changes.

**Windows threads and the GC.** Threads started by `Thread.Spawn` (and `Async.Spawn`) are now registered with the Boehm GC. That means they can allocate memory safely inside a thread, and the memory is collected.

**The default optimisation level is `-O0`.** For fast builds during development, `zevic` and `zevpm` use `-O0` by default. For releases, use `zevpm build --release` (`-O2`) or `zevic -O2`. Take particular care when measuring speed.
